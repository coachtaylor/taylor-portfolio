// 01 — About

From the front line to the product line.

No predecessor. No playbook. Just signal, prioritization, and ship.

FIG.01 Strategy → design → code, most weeks

I got into product the unglamorous way — 1,200+ support tickets a quarter, face‑to‑face with the people actually using the software. You learn what’s broken before anyone writes a ticket.

That earned the move into product ownership at a top‑10 law firm, where I’ve been building the PM practice from the ground up — roadmap discipline, discovery rituals, and stakeholder choreography from first principles, shipping software the org actually uses.

Outside of work I play and coach competitive flag football across two leagues. Same job, honestly: read the field, find the gap, run the play. Most teams don’t.
