// 01 — About

From the front line to the product line.

No predecessor. No playbook. Just signal, prioritization, and ship.

I got into product the unglamorous way — 1,200+ support tickets a quarter, face-to-face with the people actually using the software. You learn what's broken before anyone writes a JIRA ticket.

Then I built the PM function from scratch at a top-10 law firm. No predecessor, no playbook, no warm handoff. Picked roadmap discipline, discovery rituals, and stakeholder choreography from first principles — and shipped real software the org actually uses.

Outside of work I play and coach competitive flag football across two leagues. Same job, honestly: read the field, find the gap, run the play. Most teams don't.
